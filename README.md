LANTERN ROW is a round-based tactical first-person shooter in a lantern-lit night market, built on three.js for the make.fun "Make the best three.js tactical FPS" bounty ($1,000 in USDC).

Five against five, single-player against bots: plant the charge at Spice Hall (A) or Lantern Court (B), or eliminate the Lantern Watch. Money earned each round buys rifles, a scoped marksman rifle, a shotgun, armour and grenades. Bots run a team plan each round, hold lanes, call what they see and hear on the radio, and rotate on it. First to 7 rounds takes the match.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome. Click to play.

WASD to move, mouse to look, click to fire, R to reload, 1/2/3 to switch weapons, G to throw a grenade (4/5/6 to pick frag, smoke or flash), E (hold) to plant or defuse the charge, B to open the buy menu, Ctrl to crouch, Space to jump, Shift to walk, right-click to scope the DMR.
